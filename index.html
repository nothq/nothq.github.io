Why

Electron is a browser per app

Each Electron app ships its own copy of Chromium and Node.js, runs a web page per workspace and spreads it across a swarm of helper processes. Open three of them and your laptop is running three browsers to show you text.

Native means no DOM, no GC

Our apps are single Rust binaries. No JavaScript, no garbage collector, no layout engine built for documents. Every frame is drawn straight to the GPU, so they open fast and scroll at your display's refresh rate.

Pixel perfect, nothing to relearn

Sidebars, message lists, composers, menus: they match the originals. You keep your accounts and your muscle memory. You just get your RAM back.

Come build it

This is early, and that is the fun part. If you have ever watched an Electron app eat your laptop, or you want to ship real code on GPUI, there is a lot to do: platform builds, notifications, and every place we are a pixel off from the original.
